## What to keep out of the current build

Do not add model juries, criterion-specific model routing, GPU fine-tuning, live coaching/chatbots, sentiment products, production role systems or operator leaderboards to chase feature counts. Several require more paid calls, reliable identity/policy inputs or a broader product. Local inference can eliminate per-call hosted fees but still requires suitable hardware, deployment and measured RU/KZ/EN performance. No such model was installed or run here.

Retain our existing null/needs-review behavior, deterministic scoring, separate critical flags, immutable originals and explicit paid budget. Do not copy placeholder seeds, fixed scoring interpretations, reworded criterion exceptions, hard-coded audio offsets or automatic review-to-training feedback without appropriate evidence/version boundaries. Higher model/model or model/machine-label agreement is not independent correctness. More product screens and public self-reported pass counts are not an official grade.
